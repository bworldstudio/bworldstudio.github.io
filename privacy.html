Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • BWorld Studio

This privacy policy describes our policies and practices on collecting, using, and disclosing your information while using our Android applications, as well as your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

If anyone decides to use our applications, this page is meant to inform users about our policies regarding the collection, use, and sharing of personal information.

We use information in order to provide and improve our applications. You agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. Except as specified in this Privacy Policy, we will never use or share your information with anyone else.

By downloading, installing, or using any application provided by BWorld Studio, you acknowledge that you have read and agree with this privacy policy.

Information We Collect & Use

While using our applications, we may request you to provide certain information in order to offer you a better experience. We will retain any information provided and use it as described in this privacy statement.

Third-party SDKs and developer libraries integrated into our applications may collect information used to identify your device or analyze app performance.

Usage Data

When you use our applications, Usage Data may be collected automatically.

Usage Data may comprise your mobile device model, unique device identifiers, operating system version, the time and date of app usage, interaction statistics, and diagnostic crash data.

We may collect certain information automatically when you access and use our applications on your mobile device, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device you use, your mobile device unique ID, your mobile operating system, app build version, advertising identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

Information Collected While Using Our Applications

While using our applications, in order to provide specific features, we may request certain permissions with your consent:

  • Device permissions and optional location access when required for specific in-app features

This information is used strictly to deliver features of our applications, as well as to improve and customize app functionality. Information is processed locally on your device or via our integrated third-party SDK partners as described in this policy.

Use of Your Data

BWorld Studio may use data for the following purposes:

  • 1) To Provide and Maintain Our Applications: To deliver application features and monitor the stability, performance, and functionality of our apps.
  • 2) To Support App Features: To enable specific tools, capabilities, and customized settings chosen by the user within the application.
  • 3) To Contact You: When necessary, to respond to support inquiries, user feedback, or to send notifications regarding app updates, bug fixes, or security improvements.
  • 4) To Process Your Requests: To handle customer support questions and resolve technical issues.
  • 5) For Improvement & Analytics: To analyze usage trends, evaluate the effectiveness of app features and advertising, and continuously improve our applications and user experience.

Third-Party SDKs and Partners

We only share information with third-party partners and SDK providers to support our applications—such as app performance tracking, crash reporting, analytics, and advertising—as directed by BWorld Studio.

Our partners are required to use any information received through our applications strictly in compliance with applicable privacy laws and regulations.

Please review the following links to learn more about the privacy policies of the third-party providers integrated into our applications (including any opt-out procedures they may provide):

  • Google Play Services – https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase SDK – https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob – https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics – https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/terms/us/

A link to a third-party privacy policy does not mean that we endorse the linked site or platform.

Please note that we are not responsible for the independent policies and practices of third parties, such as Google LLC, operating system providers, device manufacturers, or wireless carriers, regarding any information collected independently through their platforms.

Analytics Partners

To perform app optimization and stability analysis, our analytics partners collect information such as:

  • How often and how long you use our apps
  • User interactions and in-app feature usage
  • Crash logs and device performance diagnostics
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to analyze trends and improve our applications.

Security of Your Data

We care about the security of your data, but keep in mind that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we make every effort to protect your data using commercially reasonable safeguards, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Data Deletion

We transmit diagnostic and advertising data through our apps via third-party providers such as Google Firebase, Analytics, and AdMob to track app stability and serve appropriate ads. If you would like to request deletion of any data associated with your device, please send us an email at bworldstudio@gmail.com along with your device advertising identifier (GAID), and we will request that our providers delete the data accordingly.

Children’s Privacy

We never knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 or allow them to submit personal information through our applications. Please do not submit any information about yourself (including your name, address, phone number, or email address) if you are under the age of 13. Anyone under the age of 13 is not permitted to enter personal information. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under the age of 13, we will remove it immediately. Please notify us if you believe we have received information from or about a child under the age of 13.

External Links

Our applications may contain links to external websites or third-party web pages. If you tap on a third-party link, you will be redirected to that site. We strongly recommend you review the privacy policy of each site you visit.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or external resources.

Cookies and Device Identifiers

Our Android applications do not use web cookies. However, third-party analytics and advertising partners (such as Google AdMob and Firebase) may use mobile advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and local app storage to analyze app usage, prevent fraud, and serve advertisements. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes to this policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
