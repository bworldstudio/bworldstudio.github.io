General Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • BWorld Studio Developer Team

1. Overview & Scope

This General Privacy Policy applies to all Android mobile applications, utilities, games, and software published by BWorld Studio on the Google Play Store.

At BWorld Studio, we respect your privacy and believe in building transparent, user-friendly mobile software. This policy outlines our general practices regarding device permissions, data security, and third-party services across our entire application catalog.

2. Personal Information Handling

We do not collect, harvest, sell, rent, or trade your Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

Our applications:

  • Do not require account registration, usernames, or passwords.
  • Do not ask for real names, physical addresses, phone numbers, or credit card details.
  • Do not collect biometric records, voice recordings, or facial recognition templates.
  • Do not track or store your precise real-time GPS location.

3. Device Permissions & Local Processing

To provide essential functionality, certain BWorld Studio apps may request standard Android operating system permissions on an as-needed basis.

  • Principle of Minimum Privilege: Permissions are requested only when required to deliver a specific user-initiated action (such as camera access for visual tools, storage/media access to import or save files locally, or notification access for alerts).
  • Local On-Device Execution: All core feature processing (including image manipulation, visual overlays, audio, or document handling) occurs locally and in real-time on your smartphone or tablet. No user files, photographs, media, or camera frames are ever uploaded, transmitted, or backed up to external servers.
  • User Control: You have the freedom to grant, deny, or revoke any permission at any time through your device settings (Settings > Apps > App Name > Permissions).

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep our applications available free of charge, our apps may integrate industry-standard third-party developer libraries:

  • Google Play Services: Provided by Google LLC to support app distribution, runtime libraries, and updates. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Advertisements in our free apps are served via Google AdMob in full compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies. AdMob may process non-sensitive device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) to provide relevant or non-personalized advertisements according to your device preferences. You can reset or opt-out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

5. Children's Privacy

BWorld Studio complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policies. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has inadvertently provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will delete the information without delay.

6. Data Security

We employ standard security practices to ensure our applications are safe, stable, and secure. Because our applications do not transmit user files or personal data to remote cloud servers, your personal data remains strictly on your device.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion

Because BWorld Studio does not maintain user accounts or cloud databases, there are no remote profiles to manage or delete. You can permanently erase all local application preferences and cached files at any time by clearing the application's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this General Privacy Policy periodically to reflect updates in our apps or changes in applicable Google Play policies. The latest version will always be publicly available on this page with the effective date noted above.
