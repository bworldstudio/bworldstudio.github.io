Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026 • BWorld Studio Developer Team

1. Introduction

Welcome to BWorld Studio. We develop independent mobile applications for Android, including AR Drawing – Trace & Sketch (package: com.ardrawing.trace.sketch) and other tools released under the BWorld Studio developer account on Google Play.

We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines what information our applications access, how it is used, and how we protect your personal data in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations.

2. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Our applications request only the minimum necessary permissions required to provide essential core features:

  • Camera Permission (CAMERA): Used in AR Drawing – Trace & Sketch strictly to project virtual sketch templates onto physical paper through a live camera viewfinder overlay. All camera processing happens exclusively on your device in real-time. No camera frames, photographs, videos, or biometric face data are ever recorded, saved, stored, or transmitted to any external server.
  • Storage / Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used solely to allow you to select your own photo from your device gallery to trace or sketch locally. Your personal files remain on your device and are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • Flashlight Permission (FLASHLIGHT): Used solely as an optional utility to illuminate physical paper while tracing in low-light environments.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

BWorld Studio does not operate user accounts, cloud databases, or tracking profiles. We do not collect:

  • Names, phone numbers, or physical addresses
  • Credit card or financial information
  • Biometric identifiers or facial recognition data
  • Precise GPS location data

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our free applications may incorporate industry-standard third-party SDKs to support development, including:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, updates, and foundational Android APIs. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may utilize standard mobile advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID) in accordance with Google Play's Ads Policy to serve non-personalized or personalized ads depending on your device settings. You can reset or opt-out of ad personalization at any time in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our applications comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

6. Security of Your Data

Because our apps process drawing overlays locally on your smartphone, your creative work and camera view never leave your device. We follow industry best practices to ensure our code is secure and free of unauthorized vulnerabilities.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or updates in Google Play policies. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
